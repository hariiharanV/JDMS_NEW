JDMS Next · UI review · October 2026

The product is solid. The screens make people work too hard to see it.

Reviewed from the 28 user-manual screenshots (maker, checker, records officer, administrator). Nine findings, then four redesigned screens to the right: Home, Tasks, Document and Upload.

Findings

High 01 · All screens

Top-tab navigation won't hold the full product

Makers see 5 tabs, but records officers and admins add Recycle bin, Records, Administration and Audit. A single row of tabs runs out of room, and in Arabic it gets worse. There's no global search, and the user name isn't a menu.

Fix: a grouped left sidebar (Work · Find · Governance · Admin) with task count badges, plus a global search in the top bar that understands title, CIF and document number.

High 02 · Tasks inbox & drawer

Checkers approve without seeing the document

The task drawer shows "Approval, round 1", one history line and three buttons. To judge a passport, the checker has to open the document in another view and come back. The Kind and Stage columns repeat "Approval", and Subject repeats the item text.

Fix: a split inbox. The list on the left has due-date chips and filters; the right side shows the preview, the key fields beside it and a sticky decision bar. Reject requires a reason.

High 03 · Document page

Delete is as loud as Download, and the preview is hidden on a tab

The buttons sit next to the status badges at title height, and a red-outlined Delete is one click from Download. The Details tab is a grey 4-column label/value table with 60% of the screen empty below it. You have to go to the Viewer tab to see the document at all.

Fix: two panes, preview left and facts right. One primary action, Delete moved into a "More" menu, and approval history as a timeline under the facts.

High 04 · Status & classification badges

Badges fail contrast and repeat on every row

The small green "Active" and amber "Pending approval" text on pale fills is roughly 3:1, below WCAG AA for 12px text. "Confidential" is the same outlined badge on every row, so it carries no information and pulls the eye away from status.

Fix: darker chip text (≥4.5:1) with a status dot so it isn't colour alone. Classification becomes a quiet lock icon and label, emphasised only above the bank default.

Medium 05 · Home

Home is two lists, and one of them is clipped

"My tasks (9)" shows five identical "Amend and resubmit" rows with no urgency. The Recent documents table cuts the Created column off and scrolls sideways inside its card. There's no summary of where your work stands.

Fix: status counters you can click to filter, tasks grouped by action with due chips, and a 5-column recent table that fits.

Medium 06 · Upload

The file comes last on a 20-field form

Five optional references (account, collateral, contract, transaction, application) sit between the required fields. The drop zone and the Upload button are at the bottom, and the maker isn't told who will approve the upload.

Fix: file first, required fields next and optional references folded away. A side summary shows the checks passed and the approval route before submitting.

Medium 07 · Search

Result rows are unlabelled codes

"PASSPORT HMVNNT8YN495 001 25 Oct 2026": which one is the CIF and which is the branch? Facets show raw codes (PASSPORT, CUSTOMERS) as plain text links, there's no sort, and CIF has its own search box beside the main one.

Fix: labelled metadata (CIF · Branch · Created), display names, checkbox facets with counts, sort control, and a single search box that recognises a CIF.

Medium 08 · Sign-in

Sign-in is the stock Keycloak theme

The first screen a bank sees is a dark polygon background with "JDMS NEXT (LOCAL DEVELOPMENT)" in spaced capitals and a different blue from the app.

Fix: a small custom Keycloak login theme using the same navy, logo, font and English/Arabic switch. Show the environment as a small tag, not the title.

Medium 09 · Visual system

Codes and formats leak into the UI

Raw codes appear where people read: "BANK1", "PASSPORT", "(PASSPORT)" after every task title, "001" for branch. Dates appear as both "2031-05-31" and "25 Oct 2026, 22:31". Page titles, table headers and body text are all close in weight, so nothing leads the eye.
