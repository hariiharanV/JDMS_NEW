Disposition

Controlled destruction of documents whose retention has ended. Always two officers.

  1. 1 You prepare a batch
    Documents past retention. Anything on hold is left out.
  2. 2 A second officer reviews
    They approve and destroy, or reject the batch.
  3. 3 Content is destroyed
    A record that it existed, and when it was destroyed, remains.
